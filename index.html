Privacy Policy for Transio Last updated: November 2026 Transio is a messaging translation application designed to help users communicate across language barriers. Information We Access With your explicit in-app consent, Transio accesses incoming and outgoing SMS message content, text entered by users, and microphone voice audio in order to provide real-time translation and messaging functionality. How Information Is Used SMS message content, text, and audio data are used solely to provide real-time translation and messaging features within the application. Third-Party Services To perform translations, Transio securely transmits SMS message content, user-entered text, and voice audio over encrypted HTTPS connections to Microsoft Azure Cognitive Services (Microsoft Azure Translator at api.cognitive.microsofttranslator.com). Data is shared solely for the purpose of generating translations and is never used for advertising. Data Storage and Retention Transio does not sell, rent, or trade user data. SMS message content and audio are transmitted solely for real-time translation and are not stored on Transio-operated servers. Security Reasonable technical and administrative measures, including encrypted HTTPS transmission, are taken to protect information transmitted through the application. Children's Privacy Transio is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. Contact For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play listing.
