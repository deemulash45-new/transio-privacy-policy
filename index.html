Privacy Policy for Transio Last updated: October 2026 Transio is a messaging translation application designed to help users communicate across language barriers. Information We Access Transio may access message content entered by users or received through supported messaging features in order to provide translation functionality. How Information Is Used Information is used solely to provide translation and messaging features within the application. Third-Party Services Transio may send text entered by users to third-party translation providers, such as Microsoft Azure Translator, for the purpose of generating translations. Data Storage Transio does not sell user data. Message content is not stored on Transio-operated servers. Security Reasonable measures are taken to protect information transmitted through the application. Children's Privacy Transio is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. Contact For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play listing.
